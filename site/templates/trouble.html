Trouble zone

Each address below causes one real-world problem on purpose.

  • /trouble/status/404: any status code you put in the address
  • /trouble/old-page: a permanent redirect (301)
  • /trouble/slow?seconds=3: a slow page
  • /trouble/limited: 5 requests per 10 seconds, then 429
  • /trouble/flaky: fails twice (503), then works once
  • /trouble/agent: refuses robots that hide their name (403)
  • /trouble/wrong-charset: announces the wrong text encoding
  • /trouble/messy-cards: product cards with missing parts
